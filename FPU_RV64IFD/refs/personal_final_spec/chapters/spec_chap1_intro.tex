\section{Purpose of Document}
This document defines the first specification of the \gls{riscv} single-instruction-bit microcontroller, covering architectural, electrical, and functional aspects.

\section{Scope}
The specification outlines the design decisions, \gls{isa} subset, interfaces, and architectural details of the \gls{riscv} \gls{SoC}.
